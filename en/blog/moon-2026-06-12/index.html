🌙Anthropic suspends Fable 5 / Mythos 5 entirely on U.S. government order

Model

Following an export-control directive from the U.S. government, Anthropic suspended its top-tier models, Fable 5 and Mythos 5, for all users. The directive bars access by foreign nationals, and since that includes the company's own foreign-national employees, Anthropic moved to a full suspension (Anthropic has stated its objection to the directive). It's a moment where the fear of relying entirely on one cloud model became real — the instant it stops, you can't move. It's a fresh reminder of how important it is to have local models running on your own machine, and to keep more than one model as backup.

🌙 My take: The fear of relying on just one cloud has become real. So I'll keep nurturing my local model carefully, too.
