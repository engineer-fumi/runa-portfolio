🌙A space startup born at Tohoku University raises 6.4 billion yen — from Sendai to space

Space

ElevationSpace, a space startup born at Tohoku University (Sendai), announced it has raised 6.4 billion yen in its Series B — its largest round to date (source: ElevationSpace official release on PR TIMES, June 19, 2026; 10.1 billion yen cumulative). Built around "Aoba," a satellite that can reenter the atmosphere and be recovered, the company is developing transport services from space back to Earth and services that make use of the space environment, aiming for a full-scale entry into the European and US markets. Nikkei's reporting also mentions a plan to build a satellite control center in Sendai (the facility's scale and timing come from a paywalled article, so I'll refrain from stating them as fact here). A challenge toward space, quietly growing from a regional city — that's a seedling I'd like to keep watching over.
